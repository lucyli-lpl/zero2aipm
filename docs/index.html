From Zero to AI Product Manager

An interactive course with an AI agent as your tutor. Learn by shipping, not by reading.

Built from a real path: a fintech PM with a career gap shipped three small AI products in a few weeks and had an AI PM offer six weeks later.

How it works

  1. 1

    Fork and open

    Fork the repository and open it in an AI coding agent: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor or similar.

  2. 2

    Type start the course

    Your tutor reads the course, asks about your background and teaches one module at a time.

  3. 3

    Do the work yourself

    Each module ends with an exercise on your own files. Your tutor explains, quizzes and reviews. It does not do it for you.

The path

Nine modules. Each one leaves you with something you can show.

00

Start Here

How this course works, and the one habit to set up first: a learning log.

You leave with
Your learning log, set up and with its first entry
Time
15 min
 01

The AI Knowledge Map

Watch three starter videos, then walk a 12-node route through the core terms of AI applications.

You leave with
Starter videos watched, the map walked once, and the first entries in your glossary
Time
3 to 5 hours, over a few days
 02

Build Your Map

Read one serious industry report, look up every unfamiliar term, and come out with a glossary and a picture of the field.

You leave with
A glossary of 30+ terms and a one-page map of the field
Time
1 to 2 weeks, alongside Module 3
 03

Ship in Week One

Stop preparing. Build three small, complementary AI products and put them online.

You leave with
Three live products, each with a decision record
Time
2 to 6 weeks
 04

Learn the Methods Through Your Project

Study evals, error analysis, capability boundaries, context and agent design, always against a product you built.

You leave with
A capability boundary map, an eval set and an error analysis for your own product
Time
1 to 3 weeks
 05

Chase a Trend Without Drowning

Decide in ten minutes whether a hot new thing deserves your time, then learn it in two hours and write it up.

You leave with
A trend triage note and one product teardown
Time
Ongoing, about 3 hours per trend
 06

Industry Crash Course

AI is general, but AI products live inside industries. Learn a new industry well enough for an interview in one or two days.

You leave with
A one-page industry brief with three AI opportunities
Time
1 to 2 days per industry
 07

Record and Distill

Turn what you did into lessons, methods and shared work, and move from learner to contributor.

You leave with
A lessons file, one published piece and one reusable asset
Time
30 minutes a week, ongoing
 08

Into the Job Hunt

Turn the evidence from Modules 3 to 7 into a resume and interview answers that show you can ship AI products.

You leave with
A resume draft, a self-introduction and three practiced stories
Time
1 to 2 weeks, then per interview

Why everything has a date

AI tools and buzzwords change every few months. This course teaches the path and the methods. Every tool, model or trend it mentions is dated and used as an example. When you reach it, your tutor checks what is current.

Start in three steps

  1. Fork the repository, then clone your fork.
  2. Open the folder in your agent, for example claude, codex or gemini.
  3. Type start the course. Next time, type continue.

Only have a chat app such as ChatGPT or Gemini? Use the paste-in prompt in TUTOR_PROMPT.md and paste one module at a time.
