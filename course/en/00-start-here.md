# Module 0 · Start Here

## Why this course exists

In March 2026 I was a product manager with six years of experience, a folder full of AI articles and nothing I had built. What changed things was pausing my job applications, building a few small AI products, and writing down every day what I did. Six weeks later I had an AI product manager offer. This course is the path I took, cleaned up so you can walk it faster.

Two things to know:

- **It teaches a path and methods.** AI tools and buzzwords change every few months. Anything that names a specific tool or trend carries a date; check what is current when you get there.
- **An AI is your tutor.** It explains, guides and checks your work. The exercises are yours to do.

## How it runs

1. Clone this repository and open it in an AI coding agent that can read and write files (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor and similar).
2. Type `start the course` and your tutor takes you through one module at a time. Next time, type `continue` to pick up where you left off.
3. Your work goes into the `my-journey/` folder.

Only have a chat app? Use the prompt in `TUTOR_PROMPT.md` and paste one module at a time.

## The one habit: a learning log

A plain spreadsheet, one row per item:

| Date | What I worked on | Status | Notes | Links and takeaways |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 03-13 | Customer service agent on a no-code platform | Done | | Published it. The workflow finally ran end to end. |
| 03-11 | Local RAG with Ollama and Docker | In progress | Network kept dropping | Models pulled, not wired together yet |

It shows progress on days that feel like nothing happened. The "Notes" column records why things stalled, which becomes the most useful part later. And it is the raw material for your resume and interviews.

## Get started

1. Tell your tutor your background in two or three sentences: what you do now, what you know about AI, and why you want to move into AI product work.
2. Your tutor creates `my-journey/learning-log.md`. Add your first entry: today you started this course.

That is it. On to Module 1.
